# home-cli privacy policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

home-cli is a personal tool used only by its owner's household.

**What it accesses.** With the owner's consent, home-cli uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read the status of the owner's own Nest thermostat and to change its setpoint or mode when the owner asks.

**What it stores.** The OAuth refresh token and a short-lived access token are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. Thermostat readings are not stored, logged, or kept beyond the command that requested them.

**Sharing.** No data is sold, shared with, or sent to anyone other than Google's own APIs. There are no analytics, ads, or third-party services.

**Revoking access.** Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or the Nest partner connections settings.

**Contact.** Please open an issue on this repository.
